### 🔎 AI Job Search Automation

Currently building an **AI-powered job search and application automation platform** focused on making the job-search process faster, more personalized, and less repetitive.

The current MVP is designed to work with **LinkedIn, Naukri, and Indeed**, with an architecture that can be extended to additional job platforms in the future.

The system is designed around:

- 🔍 Automated job discovery from **LinkedIn, Naukri & Indeed**
- 🧠 AI-powered **job relevance matching and ranking**
- 📄 Resume-aware matching based on skills, experience and role requirements
- 🔗 Duplicate job detection across multiple sources
- 📊 Centralized job tracking and status management
- 👤 Candidate profile and job-preference based filtering
- 📧 Recruiter/contact and email information extraction when available
- 💬 **WhatsApp cold-message generation** for recruiter outreach
- ✉️ Personalized **cold-email generation**
- 📎 Job-specific resume selection and application support
- ⏱️ Scheduled and automated job-search workflows
- 📝 Application and outreach tracking
- 🤖 AI-assisted workflows to reduce repetitive manual tasks

**Current Focus:** Python • AI/LLMs • Automation • LinkedIn • Naukri • Indeed • Backend APIs • Web Automation • PostgreSQL • Scheduling • Data Processing

> 🚧 **Currently building — MVP focused on LinkedIn, Naukri & Indeed. Additional job platforms and capabilities are planned for future versions.**
